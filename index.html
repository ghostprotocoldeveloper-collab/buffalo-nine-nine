How the job works

Each file on the desk is someone who needs a decision. Drag it left or right to answer. On a keyboard, press an arrow once to preview and again to commit.

Every answer moves your four standings. A dot above a meter shows it will change; a big dot means a big change. The job is balance. If any meter empties or fills all the way up, your career ends and you start over as a Constable. A flashing icon means a meter is close to either edge. A few answers are so bad they end your career on the spot. You'll know them when you see them. Probably.

The long game

Answers that read like real police work earn evidence on the open file. Enough evidence brings a warrant, then an arrest, then court. A conviction sends the villain to Kent Institution and opens the next file.

Evidence, medals, gear and the people you meet carry over between careers. Rank and service do not. Promotions bring new responsibilities and new kinds of files, which is a polite way of saying more problems. The higher you rise, the bigger every swing gets in both directions, and the job nudges your standings around a little every shift.

Start over

Wipes your whole career: evidence, convictions, medals, gear, endings and everyone you've met. The squad will pretend they've never seen you. Primm won't have to pretend.
