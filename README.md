# Richmond Nine-Nine

A Reigns-style card game set at Buffalo Detachment, an RCMP detachment in Richmond, British Columbia.
Swipe each file left or right to answer the squad, and keep four standings balanced:
Cases, Squad, Brass and Swagger. Let any of them empty or fill all the way up and your career is over.

Build evidence, make arrests, win in court, put five villains in Kent Institution,
and work your way up from Constable to Inspector while collecting medals, gear and contacts.

## Play

Open `index.html` in any modern browser, or play the hosted version on GitHub Pages.

- Drag the card left or right, or press an arrow key twice.
- Tap **?** for the field guide and the reset button.
- Tap the case strip for your case board, medals, rank and people met.

Progress saves in your browser.

## Built with

- [three.js](https://threejs.org/) r128 for the 3D desk and cards
- Google Fonts: Big Shoulders Display and Courier Prime
